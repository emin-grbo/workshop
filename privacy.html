Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · covers every app by Emin Grbo

The short version

No accounts, no selling of data, no ads inside the apps. I never store what you create in them. Where an app uses AI, the text or photo you give it goes to OpenAI to get the answer; Marked's photo scan and FunnelHound's App Store numbers stay on your device. A few trusted services handle purchases, crash reports and feature flags, and each is listed below with what it sees.

Who I am

The apps are made by Emin Grbo, an independent developer in Norway ("I", "me"). I am the data controller for the apps listed here. Questions go to emin.grbo@icloud.com.

FunnelHound

Your App Store Connect key and numbers

FunnelHound needs an App Store Connect API key (issuer ID, key ID and private key) that you create in App Store Connect. The key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is used only to call Apple's App Store Connect API directly from your device. Your sales, analytics, subscription and review data travel from Apple to your device and are cached there, shared with FunnelHound's own widgets on the same device. They are never sent to me or to any server of mine.

Purchases

Houndy's Pack is sold through Apple. RevenueCat checks your subscription status using an anonymous app user ID and Apple's purchase receipts. It does not receive your name or email. The ID is shown in Settings, in case you want your purchase history deleted.

Feature flags and feedback

Flagsmith receives a random identifier so the app can switch features on or off. WishKit stores the feature requests and votes you choose to submit.

Ask Houndy and AI prompts

Ask Houndy runs on Apple Intelligence on your device. AI prompts you copy contain your funnel numbers and go only where you paste them.

Support

When you email support, I receive your message and email address. Diagnostics are attached only when you choose to send them.

funnelhound.app

If you join the waitlist on the website, your email is stored with Resend to send you FunnelHound news. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Marked For Deletion

Your photos

Marked looks through your photo library on your device to find duplicates, screenshots, blurry photos and large files. Photos are never uploaded, and nothing is deleted until you confirm it.

Purchases and paywall

Subscriptions are sold through Apple. Superwall shows the paywall and records anonymous paywall events (shown, closed, purchased) and your subscription status.

Crash reports

Sentry receives crash and performance reports: device model, iOS version, app version, what the app was doing, and your IP address. Never your photos.

Advertising measurement

I measure ad campaigns with Meta's SDK. It stays off until you answer the App Tracking Transparency prompt and sends app events (such as install and purchase) to Meta only if you allow tracking. You can change this any time in the app's settings or in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Feature flags and feedback

Flagsmith receives a random identifier for feature switches. WishKit and the in-app feedback form store only what you choose to send.

AI Copywriter

What you ask it to write

To write your copy, the text, tone and options you enter are sent over an encrypted connection through a relay (AIProxy) to OpenAI's API, which generates the answer. I do not store what you write. OpenAI does not train on API requests and may keep them for up to 30 days to monitor abuse. Please don't enter anything you would not want to share.

Purchases and feedback

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. WishKit stores the feature requests and votes you choose to submit.

C.H.I.P.

Your chats

To answer you, the messages you send are sent over an encrypted connection through a relay (AIProxy) to OpenAI's API, which generates the reply. Your chat history is saved on your device. I do not store what you write. OpenAI does not train on API requests and may keep them for up to 30 days to monitor abuse. Please don't enter anything you would not want to share.

App ID and usage events

On first launch the app creates a random ID and keeps it on your device. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. The app uses it to check a block list for abuse (the list is downloaded and compared on your device) and attaches it to two events sent to LogSnag: when you switch AI model, and when you tap to buy a subscription.

Purchases and paywall

Subscriptions are sold through Apple. Superwall shows the paywall and records anonymous paywall events (shown, closed, purchased) and your subscription status.

Crash reports

Sentry receives crash and performance reports: device model, OS version, app version, what the app was doing, the layout of the screen at the time, and your IP address.

Feature flags and feedback

The app downloads feature switches and service announcements from Supabase; it sends nothing about you there. WishKit and the feedback form (Typeform, opened in your browser) store only what you choose to send.

Mushroom Identifier, PlantBro and ThingKing

Your photos

To identify a mushroom, plant or object, the photo you take or pick is sent over an encrypted connection through a relay (AIProxy) to OpenAI's API, which describes what it sees. Only that one photo is sent, never your library. I do not store your photos. OpenAI does not train on API requests and may keep them for up to 30 days to monitor abuse.

Your past scans are saved on your device. Never eat a mushroom or plant based on an app's answer. AI can be wrong, and a wrong answer can be deadly.

Purchases and feedback

Subscriptions are sold through Apple. Superwall shows the paywall and records anonymous paywall events (shown, closed, purchased) and your subscription status. WishKit stores the feature requests and votes you choose to submit.

Kenetic (DateGPT Assistant)

What you share

To suggest replies and openers, the screenshots you pick (chats or dating profiles) and the text you type are sent over an encrypted connection through AIProxy to OpenAI's API. The app only sees the photos you pick, never your whole library. Your history is saved on your device and I do not store any of it. Screenshots can show other people's names, photos and messages, so only share what you are comfortable sharing. OpenAI does not train on API requests and may keep them for up to 30 days to monitor abuse.

Purchases and feedback

Subscriptions are sold through Apple. Superwall shows the paywall and records anonymous paywall events and your subscription status. WishKit stores the feature requests and votes you choose to submit.

Keybård (Learn Norwegian)

The keyboard

Keybård asks for Full Access so the keyboard can reach the internet. It never sends what you type on its own: only when you tap the translate button is the text in the field you are typing in sent through AIProxy to OpenAI's API to translate it. iOS never lets third-party keyboards into password fields. I do not store your text.

Usage events, purchases and feedback

LogSnag receives a random device ID with app launches, paywall views and errors. Subscriptions are sold through Apple, with the paywall shown by Superwall. WishKit stores the feature requests and votes you choose to submit.

Warpo (Time Warp Scan)

The time-warp effect is made on your device from your camera. Photos and videos are saved to your library only when you save them, and are never uploaded. Subscriptions are sold through Apple, with the paywall shown by Superwall. Sentry receives crash reports: device model, OS version, app version, what the app was doing, and your IP address.

Lojalitet

Stamp cards

Your stamp cards are saved on your device and synced through your own private iCloud account; I cannot see them. The list of coffee shops is downloaded from Supabase, filtered by the country you choose.

Location and NFC

When you collect a stamp, the app checks on your device that you are near the coffee shop, and reads the shop's NFC tag. Your location is never sent anywhere.

App ID and stamp events

The app creates a random ID and keeps it on your device. When you collect a stamp or claim a reward, the shop name and that ID are sent to LogSnag so unusual activity (such as many stamps in a short time) can be spotted. The ID is also checked against a block list that is downloaded and compared on your device.

Crash reports and feedback

Sentry receives crash reports: device model, OS version, app version, what the app was doing, the layout of the screen at the time, and your IP address. WishKit stores the feature requests you choose to submit.

un:safe

TelemetryDeck receives anonymous usage signals (such as a game started or a lock opened) with a hashed, anonymous user ID. It holds no personal data and cannot identify you. Leaderboard scores go through Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, if you are signed in to it.

Older apps

music box, spendersson and Accessibility Assistant

These send crash reports to Sentry: device model, OS version, app version, what the app was doing, and your IP address. spendersson keeps your budgets in your own private iCloud account (and in the iCloud share, if you share a budget with someone); I cannot see them.

Runny Egg, Retro Boi, SVRD experience, StorkUI and Atom1c

These collect no data at all. Everything stays on your device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Norwegian law you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and object to its use. Most of it never reaches me; for what does (support emails, waitlist, purchase records tied to an app user ID), email emin.grbo@icloud.com and I will handle it within 30 days. You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version goes up on this page with a new effective date.
