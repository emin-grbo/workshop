Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · covers every app by Emin Grbo

The short version

No accounts, no selling of data, no ads inside the apps. Your photos and your App Store numbers are processed on your device and never reach me. A few trusted services handle purchases, crash reports and feature flags, and each is listed below with what it sees.

FunnelHound Marked For Deletion un:safe · Runny Egg · Retro Boi Your rights

Who I am

The apps are made by Emin Grbo, an independent developer in Norway ("I", "me"). I am the data controller for the apps listed here. Questions go to emin@grbo.space.

FunnelHound

Your App Store Connect key and numbers

FunnelHound needs an App Store Connect API key (issuer ID, key ID and private key) that you create in App Store Connect. The key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is used only to call Apple's App Store Connect API directly from your device. Your sales, analytics, subscription and review data travel from Apple to your device and are cached there, shared with FunnelHound's own widgets on the same device. They are never sent to me or to any server of mine.

Purchases

Houndy's Pack is sold through Apple. RevenueCat checks your subscription status using an anonymous app user ID and Apple's purchase receipts. It does not receive your name or email. The ID is shown in Settings, in case you want your purchase history deleted.

Feature flags and feedback

Flagsmith receives a random identifier so the app can switch features on or off. WishKit stores the feature requests and votes you choose to submit.

Ask Houndy and AI prompts

Ask Houndy runs on Apple Intelligence on your device. AI prompts you copy contain your funnel numbers and go only where you paste them.

Support

When you email support, I receive your message and email address. Diagnostics are attached only when you choose to send them.

funnelhound.app

If you join the waitlist on the website, your email is stored with Resend to send you FunnelHound news. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Marked For Deletion

Your photos

Marked looks through your photo library on your device to find duplicates, screenshots, blurry photos and large files. Photos are never uploaded, and nothing is deleted until you confirm it.

Purchases and paywall

Subscriptions are sold through Apple. Superwall shows the paywall and records anonymous paywall events (shown, closed, purchased) and your subscription status.

Crash reports

Sentry receives crash and performance reports: device model, iOS version, app version, what the app was doing, and your IP address. Never your photos.

Advertising measurement

I measure ad campaigns with Meta's SDK. It stays off until you answer the App Tracking Transparency prompt and sends app events (such as install and purchase) to Meta only if you allow tracking. You can change this any time in the app's settings or in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Feature flags and feedback

Flagsmith receives a random identifier for feature switches. WishKit and the in-app feedback form store only what you choose to send.

un:safe, Runny Egg and Retro Boi

These apps collect no data at all. Everything stays on your device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Norwegian law you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and object to its use. Most of it never reaches me; for what does (support emails, waitlist, purchase records tied to an app user ID), email emin@grbo.space and I will handle it within 30 days. You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version goes up on this page with a new effective date.
